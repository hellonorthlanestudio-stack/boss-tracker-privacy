Privacy Policy – Boss Tracker & Build Planner

Effective date: October 28, 2026

Boss Tracker & Build Planner ("the app") is published by Northlane Studio. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising.

Data stored on your device

Your boss checklist progress and stat values are saved only on your own device, using the device's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Permissions

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Third parties

We do not sell or share data with third parties. If a future version adds advertising, this policy will be updated before that version is released to describe what the advertising provider collects.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
